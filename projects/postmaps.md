PostMaps brings high-precision geolocation and last-mile routing to delivery and multi-hub operations, available as a mobile app.

## The problem

Last-mile delivery loses time and accuracy with imprecise addresses and manual routing.

## What we built

- **Precise geolocation:** accurate points for pickup and drop-off.
- **Last-mile routing:** optimized routes for delivery teams.
- **Multi-hub:** coordination across hubs and zones.
- **Mobile app:** built for drivers in the field.
