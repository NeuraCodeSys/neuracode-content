ReviveDeal lets agencies and owners publish and manage properties, short stays and developments in one place, with search, map listings, market insights and a mortgage calculator.

## The problem

Agents juggle listings across portals, spreadsheets and WhatsApp groups, with no single place to manage and sell.

## What we built

- **Listings and search:** properties with filters by city, price and type.
- **Map and rich detail:** photo galleries and a map location on every listing.
- **Stays and developments:** short-term rentals and new developments, not just sales.
- **Mortgage calculator:** buyers estimate financing right on the listing.
- **Market insights:** market data to price with confidence.
- **Agency and owner accounts:** tools for agencies, agents and individual owners.
