SportSynced runs tournaments and teams in one place, with fixtures, rosters, live scores and stats instead of spreadsheets and group chats.

## The problem

Organizers track results in three places at once while teams keep asking for scores and schedules.

## What we built

- **Tournament management:** create and run tournaments with brackets and fixtures.
- **Team rosters:** player profiles and team coordination.
- **Live scores:** results update in real time for everyone following.
- **Discovery and registration:** teams find and register for tournaments.
- **Analytics and stats:** performance tracking across the season.
- **Multi-sport:** soccer, basketball, volleyball and more.
