CodersHub connects companies with specialist engineers and helps build distributed teams that ship.

## The problem

Hiring specialist developers and assembling distributed teams is slow and hit-or-miss.

## What we built

- **Specialist hiring:** vetted engineers for specific stacks.
- **Distributed teams:** tools to assemble and coordinate remote teams.
- **Talent network:** a growing pool of technical talent.
